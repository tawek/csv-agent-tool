# ShopVibe — Site Knowledge Base

## 1. About Us

ShopVibe is an online destination for everyday essentials and passion-driven purchases. We curate products across six main categories — Electronics, Home & Kitchen, Groceries & Gourmet, Sports & Outdoors, Office Supplies, and Gaming — and sell them with straightforward pricing, fast shipping, and a no-questions-asked return window.

Our editorial voice for product pages is flexible: we support neutral descriptive copy, witty/cheeky tone, story-driven narratives, and other style presets chosen by the seller.

### Corporate Identity
- **Registered name:** VibeTrade Inc.
- **Headquarters:** Remote-first, EU-registered (Cayman Islands holding)
- **Customer-facing brand:** ShopVibe
- **Support hours:** Mon-Fri 08:00-22:00 CET; Sat-Sun 10:00-18:00 CET

## 3. Customer Policies

### 3.1 Returns & Refunds
- **Return window:** 30 calendar days from delivery.
- **Condition:** Items must be in original packaging with tags attached.
- **Process:** Initiate a return at `/help/returns`, print the pre-paid label, drop at any courier point.
- **Refund timeline:** 5-7 business days after the warehouse receives the return.
- **Exclusions:** Perishable groceries and opened gourmet consumables are non-returnable unless defective.
- **Restocking fee:** None.

### 3.2 Pricing & Price Matching
- All prices listed are final and include applicable VAT/gst where applicable.
- **Price-match guarantee:** If the same SKU is listed cheaper by an authorised competitor within 14 days of purchase, contact support for a partial refund of the difference.

### 3.3 Gift Cards & Store Credit
- Gift cards are delivered via email as a scannable code.
- Store credit can be issued as a goodwill gesture for failed deliveries or defectives.

### 3.4 Loyalty Programme — "Vibe Points"
- Earn 1 Vibe Point per €1 spent.
- Redeem 100 points = €1.00 discount on any future order.
- Points expire 12 months after earning.
- Double-point promotions run monthly on the first Friday of the month.

## 4. Rebates & Promotions

### 4.1 Seasonal Mega-Sale
- Runs Black Friday weekend (Fri-Sun).
- Up to 40 % off selected categories.
- Stackable with Vibe Points.

### 4.2 Bundle Rebates
- Buy any 2 items from the same category → 10 % off at checkout.
- Buy any 3 items across different categories → 15 % off at checkout.
- Promotional code: `BUNDLE10`, `BUNDLE15`.

### 4.3 First-Order Welcome
- New customers receive 15 % off their first order with code `WELCOME15`.
- One use per customer account & email.
- No combining with Bundle Rebates.

### 4.4 Flash Deals
- Limited-quantity deals announced on the `/products/deals` page.
- First-come, first-served; no rain checks.

### 4.5 Referral Bonus
- Existing customers share a referral link.
- Referee gets €5 off; referrer gets €5 store credit after the referee's first order ships.

## 5. Shipping & Delivery

### 5.1 Standard Rates
| Order value | Shipping charge | Delivery estimate |
|-------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Under €30   | €5.99          | 4-7 business days |
| €30 – €60   | €3.99          | 3-5 business days |
| Over €60    | Free           | 3-5 business days |

### 5.2 Express Shipping
- Flat €9.99.
- Next-business-day delivery for orders placed before 14:00 CET (EU/UK).
- 2-day delivery for REST of the world (US, CA, AU, NZ).

### 5.3 International
- All countries outside the EU see duties and taxes included at checkout (DDP).
- Tracking is available for every order regardless of destination.

### 5.4 Packaging
- Carbon-neutral shipping: all boxes are 100 % recycled cardboard.
- Optional gift wrapping at €1.99 per item (printed gift tag included).

### 5.5 Delivery Failures
- If a delivery attempt fails twice, the item is returned to the warehouse.
- A re-ship costs €2.99.

## 6. Target Audience & Marketing Angles

### 6.1 Primary Personas

| Persona | Age | Motivation | Category Affinity |
|---------|-----|------------|-------------------|
| The Busy Professional | 28-42 | Convenience, reliability, quality | Electronics, Office Supplies, Gourmet |
| The Home Chef | 30-55 | Cooking enthusiasts, foodies | Groceries, Gourmet, Home & Kitchen |
| The Fitness Enthusiast | 18-35 | Performance, aesthetics | Sports & Outdoors, Electronics |
| The Casual Gamer | 16-30 | Fun, aesthetics, status | Gaming, Electronics |
| The Eco-Conscious Buyer | 25-45 | Sustainability, ethical sourcing | Home & Kitchen, Sports & Outdoors, Groceries |

### 6.2 Marketing Angles

1. **"Everyday Upgrade"** — Frame each product as a small way to improve daily life. Works for Office Supplies, Home & Kitchen, and Electronics.
2. **"Taste the World"** — Position Groceries and Gourmet items as passport-free travel. Use storytelling about origin, farming, and tradition.
3. **"Level Up Your Setup"** — Gaming and Electronics marketing that centres on aesthetics (RGB, aluminium, portability).
4. **"Move Better"** — Sports & Outdoors messaging around performance, recovery, and outdoor lifestyle.
5. **"Sustainable by Default"** — Highlight eco-friendly materials (Bamboo Cutting Board, recycled packaging) as the norm, not the exception.

### 6.3 Channel Strategy
- **Email:** weekly roundup with a featured category + active promo code.
- **Social:** Instagram/TikTok short-form unboxings for Electronics & Gaming; recipe reels for Gourmet & Groceries.
- **Blog content:** "how-to" guides that organically reference product pages (SEO driver).

## 7. Customer Service Standards

### 7.1 Response Targets
- Email: reply within 4 hours during business hours.
- Chat: average wait time < 90 seconds.

### 7.2 Escalation Path
1. First-line support handles refunds, replacements, and tracking.
2. Second-line handles disputes, missing refunds, and logistics claims.
3. Manager escalation after 48 hours unresolved.

### 7.3 Language Support
- English (primary)
- French, German, Japanese
- Translated FAQ and help pages available.

## 8. Compliance & Legal

- GDPR-compliant data handling.
- CCPA-compliant for US customers.
- All product descriptions generated by ShopVibe or sellers must not make unsupported medical or health claims.
- Children under 16 require a parent's account to place orders.
- Alcohol and tobacco are not sold or shipped.
